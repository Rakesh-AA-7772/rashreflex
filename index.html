      How to Play

      Master the art of reaction speed

      Rules

      1. Start a Round: Click the start button to begin.
      2. Wait for Green: The screen turns red first, then green at a random time.
      3. React Fast: Click as soon as the screen becomes green. Milliseconds matter.
      4. Do Not Click Early: If you click while it is still red, the round restarts.
      5. Track Progress: Your best time and rank stay saved to your account.
