      How to Play

      Master the art of reaction speed

      📋 The Rules

      1. Start a Round: Click the "Click to get started" button to begin.
      2. Wait for Green: The screen will turn red. Stay calm and wait for it to turn green.
      3. React Fast: When the screen turns green, click as quickly as possible. Milliseconds matter!
      4. No Early Clicks: If you click while the screen is still red, you'll have to restart the round.
      5. Get Feedback: After each attempt, you'll see how your reaction compares to your personal best.

      🏆 Compete & Track

      Your best reaction time is saved to your account and displayed on the global leaderboard. Watch your statistics improve over time, see how you rank against other players, and challenge yourself to break your personal record. Every click counts!

      🔒 Your Data is Safe

      We use Firebase Authentication to keep your account secure. Your passwords are encrypted and never stored directly. Your game data is stored in Cloud Firestore and linked only to your account. You control your nickname and can change it anytime from your profile.
